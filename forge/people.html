Interested in reasoning about software?

Current Montana State students: send an email or drop by Gianforte Hall 360A to chat. Prospective students: apply to MSU's masters or PhD program and email me why you're interested in this area.

A strong background in computer science, discrete math, and/or logic is the best preparation—but I don't expect you to arrive an expert. I'm happy to teach the tools and techniques through the research itself.
